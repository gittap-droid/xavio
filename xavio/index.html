Xavio Privacy Notice

How Your Underwater Journal Stays on Your Device

This Privacy Notice describes the kinds of information that may appear inside Xavio when you keep a snorkeling and dive adventure journal, why that information is processed, which device permissions may be requested, and the choices available to you. Xavio is designed so you can use its core tools without creating an account or signing in.

Effective as of September 7, 2026

What This Notice Covers

Xavio helps you log dive spots and feelings, archive underwater photos, track marine creature encounters, note gear experiences and packing plans, save playful tide memories, keep a wishlist of dream reefs, and create memorial-style exports of your journey. This Notice applies to those in-app activities and to related device permissions.

It does not create a separate online membership profile. There is no mandatory registration or login flow for ordinary journaling.

Information That May Appear in Your Journal

Depending on what you choose to enter, Xavio may store on your device:

  • Dive chronicle details such as spot names, dates, visibility values, weather notes, feeling notes, highlight tags, depth, duration, water temperature, and optional latitude and longitude pins you place on a map.
  • Creature atlas entries including names, kinds, sighting counts, scene notes, habitat notes, behavior tags, rarity tiers, linked dive references, photos, and optional encounter coordinates.
  • Gear impression notes such as labels, categories, experience text, pitfall notes, star ratings, timestamps, and optional photos.
  • Voyage packing schemes with titles, trip styles, useful items, and redundant items.
  • Memory fragments with titles, mood tags, story text, dates, and optional photos.
  • Wishlist destinations with ocean names, region labels, expected creatures, anticipation notes, visit marks, and images.
  • A profile display name style presentation and an optional avatar image path when you choose to set one.
  • Local preference flags used to operate the App, such as whether certain first-run housekeeping steps have already occurred, and a local balance value used by in-app reward displays where that feature is present.

Xavio processes this material because you asked the App to keep a personal record of your ocean experiences. We do not require you to fill every field; empty optional fields simply remain unused.

Photos You Attach from Your Library

When you attach images to dives, creatures, gear notes, memories, wishes, or a profile avatar, or when you save an exported chronicle poster, Xavio may request access to your device photo library. Access occurs only after you respond to the system permission prompt.

Selected photos may be copied into the App's local document storage so your journal can display them later. Xavio does not need continuous background access to your entire library to function; you decide which images to include.

Map Pins and Place Marks

Some editors and detail screens let you drop or view a pin for a dive site or creature sighting. Coordinates you set are stored with the related journal entry on your device. Map imagery may be retrieved through the platform map framework on your device, which can involve network requests to map providers operated outside Xavio.

Xavio uses map tools to help you remember where an encounter felt meaningful. It is not designed as a live people-tracking or continuous background location surveillance product.

Local Keeping and Device Storage

Journal records are primarily kept using on-device storage mechanisms such as local preference storage and files in the App's documents area. That design supports use without a forced cloud account.

If you uninstall Xavio or clear its local data through your device settings, associated journal content on that device may be deleted. Consider exporting or otherwise preserving important photos outside the App if you wish to retain them independently.

Network Activity That May Occur

Core journaling does not depend on creating an online profile. Limited network activity may still occur when platform services are involved, for example when map tiles are drawn or when the operating system delivers App updates. Those transmissions are governed in part by your device platform and the providers that supply those system services.

Xavio does not sell your journal entries as a marketing list. Where information must leave the device for a platform feature to work, we aim to limit that exposure to what the feature requires.

Platform Helpers and Third-Party Pieces

Xavio runs on Apple device platforms and may rely on system components such as photo pickers, photo library save flows, and MapKit-style map views. Those components are subject to the platform provider's own terms and privacy disclosures.

We do not control how every system service logs technical diagnostics on your device. Review your device privacy settings if you wish to restrict photo access or other permissions.

Practical Safeguards

We take reasonable administrative and technical steps appropriate to a local journaling App, including storing user-selected media in the App sandbox where the platform allows and relying on device-level protections such as lock screens and operating-system encryption when enabled by you.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed against every risk. You can reduce exposure by keeping your device updated, using a strong device passcode, and avoiding sharing your unlocked device with people who should not see your journal.

How Long Entries Remain

Entries generally remain until you delete them inside Xavio or remove the App's local data. There is no separate remote retention calendar for a cloud account because ordinary use does not create one.

If you contact us about a privacy question, we may keep the correspondence long enough to respond and to meet record-keeping duties under applicable laws and relevant regulatory requirements.

Your Choices Inside the App

You may edit or delete individual dive logs, creature records, gear notes, packing lists, memories, and wishes using the App's editing flows. You may decline photo library permission; features that need a selected image may then work with reduced capability or without a custom photo.

You may clear or uninstall the App to remove local journal storage on that device. Because there is no forced sign-in, remote account erasure procedures that apply to online memberships are not part of the standard Xavio experience.

Younger Explorers

Xavio is not directed at children as a primary audience. If you allow a minor to keep a journal on a shared device, please supervise the content they add, especially photos and place pins. If you believe a minor has provided information in a manner inconsistent with applicable laws, contact us so we can help you understand available options.

When This Notice Changes

We may update this Privacy Notice to reflect product changes, permission adjustments, or clearer explanations. The effective date at the top of this page will change when a new version applies. Continued use of Xavio after an update means you acknowledge the revised Notice.

Legal Frame

This Privacy Notice is intended to be read together with applicable laws and relevant regulatory requirements governing personal data in the places where you use the App. If those laws grant you specific rights regarding access, correction, or deletion of personal data, you may exercise them by managing entries in the App and, where needed, by writing to us.

Disputes about privacy practices may be addressed through good-faith dialogue and, if necessary, before competent dispute resolution bodies as allowed by applicable laws.
